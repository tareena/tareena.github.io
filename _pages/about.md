I am an Assistant Professor at LUMS. Previously, I have worked at [Mathematica](https://www.mathematica.org/) and [Education Policy Initiative](http://www.edpolicy.umich.edu/) at [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/). I completed my Ph.D. in Economics at Georgia State University and worked at [Georgia Policy Labs](https://gpl.gsu.edu/) as a Graduate Research Assistant.

My research interests are in education, health, and development economics.
